Questions

Straight answers

The questions buyers ask before they make contact, answered without a sales line in front of them.

What is the difference between e-learning and a video?

A module carries structure, interaction and assessment. A video plays. If a video is enough, we say so.

Which platforms do you deliver to?

SCORM and AICC packages for standard LMS platforms, plus HTML5 for anything that hosts a web page.

Can existing classroom material be converted?

Yes. Existing decks, manuals and recordings are the usual starting point.

Do you build assessments and simulations?

Yes, both, including branching scenarios for decisions that have consequences.
