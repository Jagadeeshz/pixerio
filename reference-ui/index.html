How we work

Three stages, in this order

Discovery to deployment is a real process here, and it is kept. What changes is that each stage ends in something you can hold.

01

Discovery

What the business needs the work to do, who it is for, and what already exists. Scope, sequence and the decisions that need a client in the room.

Output: a written scope
02

Implementation

Design and build against that scope. Reviews at fixed points, not at the end, so a change of mind costs a conversation rather than a rebuild.

Output: approved work
03

Deployment

Launch, handover and documentation. Files, access and the maintenance position all written down before anyone moves on to the next project.

Output: a documented handover
