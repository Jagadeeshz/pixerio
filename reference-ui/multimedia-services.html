Questions

Straight answers

The questions buyers ask before they make contact, answered without a sales line in front of them.

What can be outsourced to your studio?

Script and storyboard, shoot or animation, edit, motion graphics, VFX and the cut-downs for social. A single deliverable or the whole production.

What is the turnaround time?

Set per project and written into the scope. Animation and VFX run longer than an edit, and the schedule says which stage is the long one.

Do you produce VFX as well as film?

Yes. VFX sits in this service, alongside 2D and 3D animation.

Can existing footage be reused?

Often. We review the existing material before quoting a shoot.
