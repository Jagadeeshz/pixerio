Privacy, in plain language.

What the enquiry form collects, why, and how long it is kept. Draft for legal review before launch.

What is collected

Name, company, email, telephone, the service selected and the brief you write. Nothing else is collected by the form, and no field is hidden.

Why

To reply to the enquiry. The details are not added to a marketing list and not sold.

Analytics and consent

Consent mode is preserved. Nothing beyond strictly necessary storage fires before consent is given, and the consent state is recorded.

Retention and your rights

Retention period, the data controller’s registered details and the request process are legal copy, and are marked here rather than invented.

Before launch

Retention period, controller details and the rights process need legal sign-off. This page exists so the link is never dead.
