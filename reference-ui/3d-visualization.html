Questions

Straight answers

The questions buyers ask before they make contact, answered without a sales line in front of them.

What is the process for 3D visualisation?

Concept and storyboard, then modelling, then lighting and render passes, then review with you. Each stage is approved before the next one starts.

How long does a render take?

It depends on how many views and how much detail. The estimate is written into the scope rather than published here, because a single number would be wrong for most projects.

What is 3D visualisation used for?

Presenting a product, an interior or a building before it exists, and getting a decision from people who will never see the physical thing.

Do you produce AR and VR as well?

Yes. Both sit inside this service, so the buyer reads one offer instead of two.
